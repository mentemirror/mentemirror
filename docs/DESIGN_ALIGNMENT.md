# Phase 1.1 — Mente Mirror visual alignment

This iteration keeps the new static-site architecture but moves the visual language much closer to the existing mentemirror.com website.

## Visual cues retained from the current site

- Government of India MSME / GST registration strip at the very top.
- Prominent "A team of Psychologists and Psychiatrists" utility message.
- Phone / WhatsApp contact and uppercase MAKE AN APPOINTMENT action.
- White, clean healthcare/wellness background rather than a highly app-like purple surface.
- Bright pink/magenta as the main brand accent, with orange and purple used sparingly as supporting accents.
- More traditional service-card proportions and less exaggerated corner rounding.
- Section labels such as Consulting / Testimonials / Insights-style small uppercase headings.
- Photo-led hero presentation using imagery already present in the migrated repository.
- Dark information-rich footer with Locate us / contact information and legal links.

## What deliberately remains new

- The new information architecture (For You, Understand Yourself, AI Wellness, Human Care, Family Wellness, Resources, About).
- Wellness Check and app sign-in CTAs.
- Five Wells framework.
- Mjuzi positioning.
- Understand → Connect → Learn → Practise → Improve journey.

The aim is continuity of brand recognition without reproducing the old website page-for-page.
