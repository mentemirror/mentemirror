        <span class="mm-assessment-route__label">Self-guided</span>
        <h2>Wellness Check</h2>
        <p>For structured self-reflection on areas such as stress, sleep, mood, focus or social wellbeing.</p>
        <dl><div><dt>Best when</dt><dd>You want a quick starting point.</dd></div><div><dt>What you receive</dt><dd>A focused wellbeing output for reflection.</dd></div><div><dt>Diagnosis?</dt><dd><strong>No.</strong> It is not a clinical diagnosis.</dd></div></dl>

        <span class="mm-assessment-route__label">Structured testing</span>
        <h2>Psychological Tests</h2>
        <p>For standardised or projective tools used to explore cognitive, personality, emotional or other psychological characteristics.</p>
        <dl><div><dt>Best when</dt><dd>You need a specific structured test or profile.</dd></div><div><dt>What you receive</dt><dd>Results and interpretation appropriate to the selected test.</dd></div><div><dt>Diagnosis?</dt><dd>A test result alone is not a diagnosis.</dd></div></dl>
        <a class="mm-btn mm-btn--secondary" href="{{ '/understand-yourself/psychological-tests/' | relative_url }}">Explore Psychological Tests</a>
      </article>

        <span class="mm-assessment-route__label">Human professional</span>
        <h2>Professional Assessment</h2>
        <p>For concerns that benefit from a psychologist's or psychiatrist's professional evaluation, conversation and judgement.</p>
        <dl><div><dt>Best when</dt><dd>Your concern is persistent, complex or needs human evaluation.</dd></div><div><dt>What you receive</dt><dd>Professional guidance and care recommendations; tests may be used when appropriate.</dd></div><div><dt>Diagnosis?</dt><dd>Only a qualified professional can diagnose where appropriate and within their scope.</dd></div></dl>
