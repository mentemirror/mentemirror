      <div><span class="mm-eyebrow">Our approach</span><h2>Support begins with understanding the person.</h2><p>Mente Mirror's counselling model starts with one-to-one conversation to understand the concerns and context of the individual. The aim is to support reflection and self-understanding using appropriate psychological methods rather than treating every person in the same way.</p><p>Depending on the need, the care pathway can bring together counselling, psychological assessment and consultation with a psychiatrist. The professional retains clinical judgement; AI can support continuity, reflection and approved low-risk actions around that care without changing a clinician's diagnosis or treatment decision.</p></div>
      <div class="mm-human-care-source-media"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/human-care/services-img-01.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Mente Mirror human care services" loading="lazy"><div class="mm-card"><h2>Human care, connected to the wider Mente Mirror journey</h2><p>Wellness technology can help with awareness and day-to-day support.     <div class="mm-continuity-flow" aria-label="Human therapy and AI between-session continuity">

      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="sparkles" %}</span><h3>Mjuzi support</h3><p>Check-ins, reflection and permitted tool follow-up between sessions.</p></article><i aria-hidden="true">→</i>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="chat" %}</span><h3>Next human conversation</h3><p>Relevant changes can be brought back into the professional care pathway.</p></article>
    </div>
