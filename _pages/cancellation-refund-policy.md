<h5 class="--mr-04">
<span>Payment and refund  terms</span>
</h5><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">Fees for booked services are payable according to the applicable fee schedule and payment terms shown or communicated for that service. Where appointment booking is enabled, booking and time-slot selection are handled through the Mente Mirror mobile app rather than this website. </p><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">If you need to cancel or reschedule, you can email or phone us at the contact number or email provided in the Contact us page. </p><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">Refund will be processed within 5-7 working days.</p><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">If you are late by 15 mins, the session will be treated as cancelled from your side.</p><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">For single session appointments: Only Rs50/- processing charges will be deducted if cancelled / rescheduled prior to 3 days of your appointment. Appointments cannot be cancelled or rescheduled within the last 72 hrs. </p><br/>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">For multi session packages, </p><br/>
<ul>
<li>If cancellation is done before the 1st session, only Rs50/- processing charges will be deducted if cancelled / rescheduled prior to 3 days of your appointment. Appointments cannot be cancelled or rescheduled within the last 72 hrs. </li>
<li>If cancellation is done after the program module has commenced, proportionate amount for the remaining sessions will be refunded as per the running installment.</li>
</ul>
<p class="appointment" style="text-align: justify;">We value your time and hence take utmost care not to reschedule your appointments from our side. In any emergency / unforeseen circumstances / technological failures, we will intimate you, should there be a need. In such circumstances, the counsellor will work out a mutually workable time to both of your conveniences.</p><br/>
