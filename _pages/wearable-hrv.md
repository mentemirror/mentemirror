<section class="mm-page-hero mm-ai-detail-hero">
  <div class="mm-container"><span class="mm-eyebrow">Smart Wearable Insights</span><h1>HRV</h1><p>Heart Rate Variability (HRV) describes variation in the time between heartbeats. When a supported wearable provides it, the trend can add context to recovery and physiological load.</p></div>
</section>
<section class="mm-section">
  <div class="mm-container mm-ai-detail-layout">
    <div><div class="mm-section-head"><span class="mm-eyebrow">Use the trend</span><h2>One signal within a bigger picture.</h2></div>
      <div class="mm-ai-point-grid">
        <article><h3>Personal baseline</h3><p>HRV differs substantially from person to person, so your own longer-term pattern matters more than comparison with others.</p></article>
        <article><h3>Recovery context</h3><p>Changes may be viewed alongside sleep, activity and how recovered you feel.</p></article>
        <article><h3>Daily load</h3><p>HRV can be one piece of context when reflecting on periods of higher physiological or emotional load.</p></article>
        <article><h3>Check-ins</h3><p>Pair wearable trends with your own report rather than allowing a device metric to define how you feel.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
