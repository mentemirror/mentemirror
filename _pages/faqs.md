    <div class="mm-faq-list">
      <details class="mm-faq-item" open>
        <summary>What is online counselling?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>It is live video counselling from a private space, using a computer, reliable internet and a quiet setting.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>How do I know if I need counselling or psychotherapy?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>Counselling can support a crisis, clarify concerns, change recurring patterns, untangle personal issues or encourage self-development.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>What qualifications do the providers have?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>Mente Mirror states its providers are qualified and experienced psychologists, counsellors, doctors and psychiatric-medication specialists.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>How long does therapy last?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>Duration depends on the need: some concerns may take a few sessions, while longer behavioural work can continue for months.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>How do I know if treatment will be beneficial?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>The existing FAQ describes psychotherapy as effective across many mental and behavioural-health concerns, with support tailored to individual needs.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>How secure is online counselling?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>The existing FAQ describes online consultations as confidential and protected through encrypted communication.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>What are the charges and fees?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>Fees vary by service, with packages and phased payments available for some longer therapies.</p>
          <div class="mm-faq-links" aria-label="Relevant service pages">
            <a href="{{ '/human-care/psychologists/' | relative_url }}">Psychologists</a>
            <a href="{{ '/understand-yourself/psychological-tests/' | relative_url }}">Psychological Tests</a>
            <a href="{{ '/human-care/psychiatrists/' | relative_url }}">Psychiatrists</a>
          </div>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>Is any special software required for online counselling?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>Sessions can use common video-calling platforms; a suitable device, good internet and a quiet private space are important.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>What types of issues can be treated online?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>The source lists anxiety, workplace and relationship concerns, depression, self-esteem, trauma, OCD, eating concerns, phobias, addiction, abuse and grief.</p>
          <p>It also notes online therapy is generally unsuitable when someone is actively suicidal or experiencing psychosis.</p>
        </div>
      </details>

      <details class="mm-faq-item">
        <summary>Will I always get the same therapist?</summary>
        <div class="mm-faq-item__answer">
          <p>The FAQ says clients normally continue with the same therapist, although a change can be requested when preferred.</p>
        </div>
      </details>
