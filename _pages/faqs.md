    <section class="mm-faq-category" id="counselling">
      <div class="mm-faq-category__head"><span class="mm-eyebrow">01</span><h2>Counselling &amp; therapy</h2></div>
      <div class="mm-faq-list">
        <details class="mm-faq-item" open><summary>What is online counselling?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Online counselling allows you to meet a counsellor or psychologist remotely, usually by live video. It can provide many of the same opportunities for conversation, reflection and therapeutic work as an in-person session while allowing you to join from a private location.</p><p>You will need a reliable internet connection, a suitable device and a quiet, private space. The most appropriate format depends on your needs and can be discussed with the professional.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>How do I know if I need counselling or psychotherapy?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>You do not need to wait for a crisis. People seek counselling for stress, relationship or family concerns, academic or career pressures, difficult life changes, emotional patterns, or simply to understand themselves better.</p><p>A first conversation can help clarify what you are experiencing and whether counselling, assessment, another professional, or a different form of support is appropriate.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>How long does therapy last?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>There is no single duration that is right for everyone. Some people work on a focused concern over a small number of sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support. The number and frequency of sessions should be guided by your goals, progress and the professional's assessment.</p><p>A routine counselling session is typically scheduled for about 40–50 minutes. Initial assessments or specialist consultations may require more time.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>How do I know if treatment will be beneficial?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Counselling and psychotherapy can be helpful for many emotional, behavioural and mental-health concerns, but outcomes vary between people. Progress depends on factors such as the concern being addressed, the therapeutic relationship, goals and engagement with the process.</p><p>Your professional should review progress with you and discuss changes to the care plan when needed.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>What kinds of concerns may be suitable for online counselling?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Online counselling may be suitable for concerns such as stress, anxiety, low mood, relationship difficulties, workplace pressure, low self-esteem, grief, adjustment to life changes, family concerns and some behavioural or coping difficulties.</p><p>Suitability should be considered individually. Online wellness or routine counselling is not a substitute for emergency care. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, use the <a href="{{ '/urgent-help/' | relative_url }}">urgent-help resources</a>.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>Will I get the same therapist each time?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Continuity with the same therapist is generally preferred because it supports an ongoing therapeutic relationship. Appointment availability can vary. If you would like to change professionals, discuss this with the Mente Mirror team so that the transition can be handled thoughtfully.</p></div></details>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="mm-faq-category" id="professionals">
      <div class="mm-faq-category__head"><span class="mm-eyebrow">02</span><h2>Professionals &amp; care</h2></div>
      <div class="mm-faq-list">
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>Who provides counselling and psychiatric care?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Mente Mirror's Human Care ecosystem includes psychologists, counsellors and psychiatrists. Their roles are different: psychological professionals provide counselling and assessment, while psychiatrists provide medical evaluation and may prescribe medication when clinically appropriate.</p><p>See the <a href="{{ '/about/team/' | relative_url }}">Team</a>, <a href="{{ '/human-care/psychologists/' | relative_url }}">Psychologists</a> and <a href="{{ '/human-care/psychiatrists/' | relative_url }}">Psychiatrists</a> pages for the professional information currently published by Mente Mirror.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>When might a psychiatrist be involved?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>If a person's needs call for medical evaluation in addition to counselling or psychological support, psychiatric consultation can become part of the care pathway. The psychiatrist can carry out a medical assessment and determine whether medication or other medical treatment is appropriate.</p></div></details>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="mm-faq-category" id="privacy">
      <div class="mm-faq-category__head"><span class="mm-eyebrow">03</span><h2>Privacy &amp; online sessions</h2></div>
      <div class="mm-faq-list">
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>How is privacy handled?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>Mente Mirror treats privacy and confidentiality as important parts of the care experience. Data handling and website practices are described in the <a href="{{ '/about/safety-privacy/' | relative_url }}">Privacy Policy</a>.</p><p>For online sessions, use a private location and a device you trust. If a third-party communication platform is used, its own privacy and security terms also apply.</p></div></details>
        <details class="mm-faq-item"><summary>Do I need special software for online counselling?</summary><div class="mm-faq-item__answer"><p>You generally need a phone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, a reliable internet connection and a quiet private space. The Mente Mirror team can confirm the video or communication platform for your appointment.</p><p>Position your device so that you can speak comfortably without interruptions and use headphones if that helps protect privacy in your environment.</p></div></details>
      </div>
