<section class="mm-section mm-section--soft">
  <div class="mm-container">
    <div class="mm-section-heading mm-section-heading--center"><span class="mm-eyebrow">Consent by purpose</span><h2>One service should not mean one blanket permission.</h2><p>The product specification separates important permissions so optional uses do not silently become part of essential service consent.</p></div>
    <div class="mm-consent-grid">
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="chat" %}</span><h3>Core service</h3><p>Information needed to deliver the service, with purpose, access and retention explained.</p></article>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="microphone" %}</span><h3>Recording</h3><p>Voice or video recording requires a separate opt-in before recording begins where the feature is used.</p></article>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="watch" %}</span><h3>Wearables</h3><p>Wearable or smartphone data should use separate permission by source and purpose, with the ability to revoke connection.</p></article>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="users" %}</span><h3>Clinical review</h3><p>Sharing with a psychologist or clinical reviewer should be clearly disclosed within the selected care pathway.</p></article>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="family" %}</span><h3>Caregiver sharing</h3><p>Recipient, scope and alert type should be specific; caregiver access is not blanket transcript access.</p></article>
      <article><span>{% include mm-icon.html name="settings" %}</span><h3>Product improvement</h3><p>Use for model training or product improvement should be a separate explicit choice rather than a condition of essential service.</p></article>
    </div>
    <p class="mm-capability-note">Exact consent controls depend on the implemented product flow and applicable law. The final production privacy notice and consent screens should match the actual data flows.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="mm-section">
  <div class="mm-container">
    <div class="mm-section-heading mm-section-heading--center"><span class="mm-eyebrow">AI memory &amp; returning sessions</span><h2>Continuity should be useful, minimal and correctable.</h2><p>Mjuzi is designed to retrieve only relevant, authorised context for the current purpose—not to recite your history or turn AI speculation into a permanent profile.</p></div>
    <div class="mm-control-grid">
      <article><strong>Approved continuity</strong><p>Goals, preferences, tools and approved summaries may support returning sessions when relevant, permitted and fresh enough to use.</p></article>
      <article><strong>Restricted records stay restricted</strong><p>Private clinician notes, detailed safeguarding records and internal risk material are not ordinary conversational memory.</p></article>
      <article><strong>Current information wins</strong><p>If you say something has changed, the system should not present older context as more authoritative than your current account.</p></article>
      <article><strong>Dispute stops ordinary use</strong><p>When an eligible memory item is disputed or excluded, the design requires it to stop appearing in ordinary AI retrieval while the record is handled appropriately.</p></article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
