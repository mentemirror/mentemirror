      <h1>Get Wild With Your Creativity</h1>
      <p>Imagination is presented as an important part of how we explore possibilities beyond what we already know.</p>
    </div>
  </header>

  <section class="mm-section">
    <div class="mm-container mm-blog-copy">
      <p>The existing Mente Mirror blog introduces this article with Albert Einstein's well-known observation that imagination can be more important than knowledge. The emphasis is on creativity as the ability to move beyond familiar answers and allow the mind to explore possibilities.</p>

      <p>Knowledge gives us material to work with; imagination helps us rearrange, question and extend that material. Creative thinking can therefore be relevant not only to art, but also to learning, problem-solving and finding new ways to respond to everyday situations.</p>

      <div class="mm-blog-takeaways">
        <article><strong>Make room for imagination</strong><p>New possibilities are easier to notice when we give ourselves permission to explore before judging an idea.</p></article>
        <article><strong>Look beyond the familiar</strong><p>Creativity often begins by asking whether a problem, experience or assumption can be viewed differently.</p></article>
        <article><strong>Use ideas actively</strong><p>Imagination becomes useful when ideas are expressed, tested, discussed or turned into something tangible.</p></article>
      </div>
